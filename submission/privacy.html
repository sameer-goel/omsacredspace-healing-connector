Privacy Policy — Om Sacred Space Healing Assistant

Last updated: 4 June 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Om Sacred Space Healing assistant (the "app"), a ChatGPT connector operated by Om Sacred Space. It explains what the app does and does not do with information.

What the app does

The app answers questions about sound healing, energy work, self-healing, and Om Sacred Space's offerings, using a fixed, pre-written knowledge base. It is an informational assistant only.

Personal data we collect

None. The app does not require an account or login, does not request or store personal data, and does not build user profiles. It only receives the specific question text you choose to send through ChatGPT in order to return a relevant answer.

How your question is used

Your question is used solely, in the moment, to match and return a relevant answer from our knowledge base. We do not retain your questions, associate them with any identity, log them for tracking, or use them for advertising or profiling.

Data we never collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details.
  • No health records or protected health information.
  • No payment or financial information.
  • No precise location, government identifiers, or credentials.
  • No chat history beyond the single question sent to the app.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect it. Your interaction with ChatGPT itself is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy.

Data retention

Because no personal data is collected or stored, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Standard, non-identifying server operational logs (if any) are kept only as needed to keep the service running and secure.

Your controls

You can stop using the app at any time by removing the connector in your ChatGPT settings. Since no personal data is stored, no deletion request is necessary.

Wellness disclaimer: The Om Sacred Space Healing assistant provides complementary wellness information. It is not medical, psychological, or professional health advice, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. For medical concerns — and especially if you are pregnant or have epilepsy or a serious mental-health condition — please consult your healthcare provider.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Email contact@omsacredspace.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
